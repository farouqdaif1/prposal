# Public Assets

Place your media files here:

## Music
- `song.mp3` - Your favorite song (will play after YES is clicked)
- Or `romantic-music.mp3` / `romantic-music.ogg` as alternatives

## Images

### Hero Background
- `couple-photo.jpg` - Your romantic couple photo for the hero section

### Photo Gallery
Create a `memories/` folder and add:
- `memory-1.jpg`
- `memory-2.jpg`
- `memory-3.jpg`
- `memory-4.jpg`
- `memory-5.jpg`
- `memory-6.jpg`

Or update the image paths in `src/components/OurStory.jsx` to match your file names.
